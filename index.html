The Studio

Our style is
your style, elevated.

Nook Interiors draws from years of creativity, global resources, and a trusted network of craftspeople to deliver spaces that are functional, beautiful, and deeply personal.

Based in Alamo, California, we serve the Bay Area and take select projects nationwide. Whether you're planning a new build, a full remodel, or simply a refresh — we're here to guide you every step of the way.

What we offer

Full-service design,
from vision to installation.

Every engagement is tailored to how you live — and who you want to become in your space.

Full Interior Design

End-to-end design for residential spaces — concept through completion. Space planning, material selection, custom furniture, art curation, and installation management.

Kitchen & Bath

Specialist focus on the rooms where material decisions carry the most weight. Cabinetry, stone, hardware, lighting — designed with precision.

Bespoke Furnishings

Custom furniture and millwork designed for your exact space, sourced from ateliers we trust. Pieces that can only be made — not found.

New Construction

Collaboration from the architectural phase forward. We work alongside your builder so interiors and structure are designed together, not retrofitted.

Renovation Consulting

Strategic guidance for homeowners navigating a renovation — helping you make the right decisions before they become permanent ones.

Staging & Refresh

Targeted interventions — lighting, art, textiles, arrangement — that transform how a room feels without requiring a full redesign.

How we work

A considered process,
from first call to final reveal.

We move deliberately. Every phase builds on the last — no shortcuts, no surprises.

Discovery

We begin with a conversation. How you live, what you love, what you've always wished your home felt like.

Concept

A curated direction — palette, material story, spatial mood — presented as a cohesive vision before anything is purchased.

Development

Detailed drawings, custom specifications, and sourcing. Every decision made with intention, presented with clarity.

Installation

We manage every delivery, every trade, every detail — so your reveal is truly a reveal.
